# 📘 School Bus Management System – User Manual

This guide explains how to use the School Bus Management System application for **morning**, **day**, and **college** shifts.

---

## 🖥 Starting the Application
1. **Open the App**  
   - Launch the application by double-clicking the desktop shortcut or running it from the start menu.
2. **Login** *(if enabled)*  
   - Enter your username and password to access the dashboard.

---

## 📂 Main Dashboard
The dashboard gives you quick access to:
- **Manual Assignment** (Morning / Day / College)
- **Automatic Assignment** (Morning / Day / College)
- **Route Management**
- **Print Assignments**
- **Reset Assignments**

---

## 📝 Manual Assignment
Manual mode allows you to choose exactly which bus goes to which stand.

### Steps:
1. Select the desired shift: **Morning**, **Day**, or **College**.
2. Choose a bus from the **Bus Dropdown**.
3. Choose a stand from the **Stand Dropdown**.
4. Click **Assign**.
5. Your selection will appear in the assignment table.
6. **Edit** – Click the edit icon to change the bus or stand.
7. **Remove** – Click the trash icon to remove an assignment.

---

## ⚡ Automatic Assignment
Automatic mode assigns buses to stands using the system’s logic.

### Steps:
1. Open the **Automatic Assignment** screen.
2. Select the shift (**Morning**, **Day**, or **College**).
3. Click **Run Automation**.
4. The system will automatically assign buses and hide them from other shifts until reset.

---

## 🔄 Reset Assignments
If you want to start over:
1. Go to the shift page (Manual or Automatic).
2. Click **Reset**.
3. All buses will be unassigned and available again.

---

## 🖨 Printing Assignments
To generate a printed list:
1. Go to the shift where the assignments are ready.
2. Click **Print**.
3. Choose your printer and settings.
4. Click **Print** to get the sheet.

---

## 🎨 Color Codes
- **Green Stand** → Assigned stand in current shift.
- **Gray Stand** → Unassigned.
- **Pink Stand** → Already assigned in another shift.

---

## 💡 Tips
- **Avoid duplicates**: Once a bus is assigned in one shift, it won’t appear in other shifts until reset.
- **Plan routes first**: Make sure routes are created in Route Management before assigning.
- **Automation works best** when buses and stands are balanced in number.
